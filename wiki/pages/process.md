## Definition
subject to a process or treatment, with the aim of readying for some purpose, improving, or remedying a condition

## Examples
- process cheese _(wordnet)_
- process hair _(wordnet)_

## Connections

- advanced_form: [[condition]] — Use 'condition' when the process treats something to achieve a specific state or quality, often for long-term preservation.
- advanced_form: [[cure]] — Use 'cure' when the process involves controlled aging or hardening to enhance strength or durability.
