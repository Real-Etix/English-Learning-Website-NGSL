## Definition
at risk of or subject to experiencing something usually unpleasant

## Examples
- 1748. David Hume. Enquiries concerning the human understanding and concerning the principles of moral. London: Oxford University Press, 1973. § 34. _(dictionaryapi)_
- A man liable to heart disease. _(dictionaryapi)_
- Someone is liable to slip on your icy sidewalk. _(dictionaryapi)_
