## Definition
change location

## Examples
- move, travel, or proceed, also metaphorically _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- synonym: [[move]]
- advanced_form: [[journey]] — Use 'journey' when you want to emphasize the process and distance of traveling, not just the act of moving from one place to another.
- advanced_form: [[traverse]] — Use 'traverse' when the travel involves crossing a specific geographic feature like a mountain, desert, or forest, highlighting the challenge or path.
- advanced_form: [[trek]] — Use 'trek' when the travel is slow, demanding, and often on foot, emphasizing effort and endurance rather than speed.
