## Definition
leisure time away from work devoted to rest or pleasure

## Examples
- we took a short holiday in Puerto Rico _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- synonym: [[vacation]]
- advanced_form: [[vacation]] — Use 'vacation' instead of 'holiday' in American English or for a planned trip away for rest.
- advanced_form: [[leave]] — Use 'leave' instead of 'holiday' when referring to official, often paid, time off from work.
- advanced_form: [[retreat]] — Use 'retreat' instead of 'holiday' for a focused period of rest, reflection, or recovery, often with a specific purpose.
