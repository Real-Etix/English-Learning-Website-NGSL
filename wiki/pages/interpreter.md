## Definition
someone who mediates between speakers of different languages  

## Examples
- A Japanese man who is tried before a German court is assisted by an interpreter in making oral statements. _(dictionaryapi)_
- Programs written in the BASIC language are usually run through an interpreter, though some can be compiled. _(dictionaryapi)_
