## Definition
the social force that binds you to the courses of action demanded by that force

## Examples
- every right implies a responsibility _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- synonym: [[duty]]
- synonym: [[obligation]]
- advanced_form: [[accountability]] — Use 'accountability' when emphasizing answerability or blame for outcomes, rather than duty.
- advanced_form: [[liability]] — Use 'liability' when focusing on legal or financial responsibility, often with negative consequences.
- advanced_form: [[onus]] — Use 'onus' when stressing that a specific duty or burden falls on a particular person.
- builds_on: [[obligation]] — Use 'responsibility' for a duty or task that is expected of you, broader than a mere obligation.
