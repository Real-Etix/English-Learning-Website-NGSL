## Definition
an electric railway operating below the surface of the ground (usually in a city)

## Examples
- in Paris the subway system is called the `metro' and in London it is called the `tube' or the `underground' _(wordnet)_

- advanced_form: [[metro]] — Use 'metro' when referring to urban rail systems in many European and Asian cities; it sounds more global and modern than 'subway'.
- advanced_form: [[underground]] — Use 'underground' especially for the London system or as a formal term for any subway; it carries a slightly more official or British tone.
