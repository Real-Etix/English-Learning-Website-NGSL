## Definition
experiencing or showing sorrow or unhappiness

## Examples
- feeling sad because his dog had died _(wordnet)_
- Better by far that you should forget and smile / Than that you should remember and be sad- Christina Rossetti _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- antonym: [[glad]]
- advanced_form: [[melancholy]] — Use 'melancholy' for a thoughtful or pensive sadness, often with a touch of nostalgia or gloom.
- advanced_form: [[dejected]] — Use 'dejected' when sadness comes from disappointment or discouragement.
- advanced_form: [[morose]] — Use 'morose' for a glum, sullen, and withdrawn type of sadness.
