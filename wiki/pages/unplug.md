## Definition
pull the plug of (electrical appliances) and render inoperable

## Examples
- To save electricity, I unplug my computer when I am not at home. _(dictionaryapi)_
- I had to unplug the sink the other day: there was far too much hair and gunk down there. _(dictionaryapi)_
