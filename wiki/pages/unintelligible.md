## Definition
poorly articulated or enunciated, or drowned by noise

## Examples
- unintelligible speech _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- domain: academic
- advanced_form: [[inarticulate]] — Use 'inarticulate' when someone cannot speak clearly or express themselves well, often due to nerves or lack of eloquence, whereas 'unintelligible' focuses on the sound being unclear.
- advanced_form: [[incoherent]] — Use 'incoherent' when speech or writing lacks logical connection, meaning it is not just hard to hear but also lacks sense, while 'unintelligible' is more about the difficulty of hearing or deciphering.
