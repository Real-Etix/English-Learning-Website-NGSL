## Definition
less than normal in degree or intensity or amount

## Examples
- low prices _(wordnet)_
- the reservoir is low _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- antonym: [[high]]
- advanced_form: [[scant]] — Use 'scant' to emphasize that something is barely enough or notably lacking; it is more formal than 'low' or 'little' and often suggests insufficiency.
- advanced_form: [[meager]] — Use 'meager' to describe an amount that is inadequate or poor, often with a tone of disappointment or hardship; stronger and more vivid than 'low'.
- advanced_form: [[negligible]] — Use 'negligible' for amounts or effects so small they can be ignored; common in formal, scientific, or analytical contexts, more precise than 'low'.
