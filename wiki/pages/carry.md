## Definition
move while supporting, either in a vehicle or in one's hands or on one's body

## Examples
- You must carry your camping gear _(wordnet)_
- carry the suitcases to the car _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- synonym: [[transport]]
- advanced_form: [[transport]] — Use 'transport' when moving items over a distance, especially in a vehicle or as part of a system, more formal than 'carry'.
- advanced_form: [[convey]] — Use 'convey' for moving something continuously or through a medium, often in a technical or formal context.
- advanced_form: [[bear]] — Use 'bear' for carrying something heavy or with effort, often in literary or formal English.
