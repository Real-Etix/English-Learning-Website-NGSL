## Definition
descended from a common ancestor but through different lines

## Examples
- Besides the arteries blood streams through numerous veins we call collaterals. _(dictionaryapi)_
- Although not a direct cause, the border skirmish was certainly a collateral incitement for the war. _(dictionaryapi)_
- Uncles, aunts, cousins, nephews and nieces are collateral relatives. _(dictionaryapi)_
