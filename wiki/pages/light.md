## Definition
of comparatively little physical weight or density

## Examples
- a light load _(wordnet)_
- magnesium is a light metal--having a specific gravity of 1.74 at 20 degrees C _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- antonym: [[heavy]]
- advanced_form: [[buoyant]] — Use 'buoyant' when something is light enough to float or rise, emphasizing its ability to stay on top of water or air.
- advanced_form: [[feathery]] — Use 'feathery' to describe something extremely light and soft, like a feather, often for texture as well as weight.
- advanced_form: [[gossamer]] — Use 'gossamer' for something delicately light and thin, often transparent or wispy, to imply both lightness and fragility.
