## Definition
rise and move, as in waves or billows

## Examples
- Toaster sales surged last year. _(dictionaryapi)_
- A ship surges forwards, sways sideways and heaves up. _(dictionaryapi)_
- He felt a surge of excitement. _(dictionaryapi)_
