## Definition
force somebody to do something

## Examples
- I am obliged to report to the police station every week. _(dictionaryapi)_
- He obliged me by not parking his car in the drive. _(dictionaryapi)_
- I am obliged to you for your recent help. _(dictionaryapi)_
