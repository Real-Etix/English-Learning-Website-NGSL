## Definition
the tendency of a body to return to its original shape after it has been stretched or compressed

## Examples
- If the sales of an item drop by 5% when the price increases by 10%, its price elasticity is -0.5. _(dictionaryapi)_
- Her elasticity allowed her to recover quickly. _(dictionaryapi)_
