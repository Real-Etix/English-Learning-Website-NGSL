## Definition
of or relating to or consisting of muscle

## Examples
- muscular contraction _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- domain: fitness
- advanced_form: [[brawny]] — Use 'brawny' to emphasize a large, strong, and heavily built muscularity, often suggesting raw power rather than definition.
- advanced_form: [[sinewy]] — Use 'sinewy' to describe a lean, wiry muscularity with visible tendons and strength, often associated with endurance or agility.
- builds_on: [[athletic]] — Use 'muscular' when emphasizing physical strength and developed muscles, while 'athletic' is broader and includes skill, agility, and participation in sports.
