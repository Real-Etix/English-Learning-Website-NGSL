## Definition
serving or used in place of another

## Examples
- an alternative plan _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- synonym: [[alternate]]
- synonym: [[substitute]]
- advanced_form: [[surrogate]] — Use 'surrogate' when referring to a person or thing that takes the place of another, especially in a functional or representative role.
- advanced_form: [[proxy]] — Use 'proxy' when referring to a person or thing authorized to act for another, often in voting or digital contexts.
- advanced_form: [[alternate]] — Use 'alternate' as an adjective meaning 'serving as a substitute' especially in American English; note that in British English, 'alternate' often means 'every other', so clarify context.
