## Definition
possessing or displaying a distinctive feature to a heightened degree

## Examples
- intense heat _(wordnet)_
- intense anxiety _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- antonym: [[mild]]
- builds_on: [[deep]] — Use 'intense' when the strength is great, often for feelings or conditions, more formal than 'deep'.
- advanced_form: [[acute]] — Use 'acute' for sudden, sharp intensity, especially of physical sensations or emotions.
- advanced_form: [[profound]] — Use 'profound' for deep, intense feelings or effects that are not necessarily sudden but have great depth.
- advanced_form: [[vehement]] — Use 'vehement' for intense, forceful expression of emotion or opinion, often in speech or action.
