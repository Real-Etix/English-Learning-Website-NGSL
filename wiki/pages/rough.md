## Definition
having or caused by an irregular surface

## Examples
- trees with rough bark _(wordnet)_
- rough ground _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- antonym: [[smooth]]
- advanced_form: [[coarse]] — Use 'coarse' to describe a texture that is rough and granular, often for materials like sandpaper or wool.
- advanced_form: [[uneven]] — Use 'uneven' when the roughness comes from an irregular or bumpy surface rather than a gritty texture.
- advanced_form: [[rugged]] — Use 'rugged' for a rough, wild, and often rocky natural surface, implying strength or harshness.
