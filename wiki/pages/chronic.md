## Definition
being long-lasting and recurrent or characterized by long suffering

## Examples
- chronic unemployment; chronic poverty; chronic anger; chronic life _(dictionaryapi)_
- chronic cough; chronic headache; chronic illness _(dictionaryapi)_
- Chronic patients must learn to live with their condition. _(dictionaryapi)_
