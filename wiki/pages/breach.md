## Definition
a failure to perform some promised act or obligation  

## Examples
- They breached the outer wall, but not the main one. _(dictionaryapi)_
- A clear breach is when the waves roll over the vessel without breaking. A clean breach is when everything on deck is swept away. _(dictionaryapi)_
