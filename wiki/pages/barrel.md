## Definition
a tube through which a bullet travels when a gun is fired  

## Examples
- a cracker barrel _(dictionaryapi)_
- the barrel of a windlass;  the barrel of a watch, within which the spring is coiled. _(dictionaryapi)_
- Throw it into the trash barrel. _(dictionaryapi)_
