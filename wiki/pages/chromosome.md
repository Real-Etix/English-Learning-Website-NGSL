## Definition
a threadlike strand of DNA in the cell nucleus that carries the genes in a linear order

## Examples
- humans have 22 chromosome pairs plus two sex chromosomes _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- domain: academic
- advanced_form: [[chromatid]] — Use 'chromatid' instead of 'chromosome' when you need to specify one of the two identical halves of a replicated chromosome, especially during cell division.
- advanced_form: [[chromatin]] — Use 'chromatin' instead of 'chromosome' when referring to the complex of DNA and proteins that makes up chromosomes in its uncondensed, functional state within the nucleus.
