## Definition
an automatic advance to the next round in a tournament without playing an opponent

## Examples
- he had a bye in the first round _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- synonym: [[pass]]
- domain: toeic
- domain: fitness
- advanced_form: [[walkover]] — Use 'walkover' when the advancement is forced by the opponent not showing up, not by the tournament structure.
- advanced_form: [[exemption]] — Use 'exemption' when the bye results from a player's special status or seed, not random draw.
