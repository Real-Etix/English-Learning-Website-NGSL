## Definition
able to be compared or worthy of comparison  

## Examples
- An elephant is comparable in size to a double-decker bus. _(dictionaryapi)_
- Six and forty-two are comparable in the divides order, but six and nine are not. _(dictionaryapi)_
- "Big" is a comparable adjective, since it can take the forms "bigger" and "biggest"; but "unique" is not comparable, except in disputed, but common, usage. _(dictionaryapi)_
