## Definition
the mechanical advantage gained by being in a position to use a lever  

## Examples
- A crowbar uses leverage to pry nails out of wood. _(dictionaryapi)_
- Try using competitors’ prices for leverage in the negotiation. _(dictionaryapi)_
- Leverage is great until something goes wrong with your investments and you still have to pay your debts. _(dictionaryapi)_
