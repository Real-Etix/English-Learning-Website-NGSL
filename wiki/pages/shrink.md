## Definition
wither, as with a loss of moisture

## Examples
- The dryer shrank my sweater. _(dictionaryapi)_
- This garment will shrink when wet. _(dictionaryapi)_
- Molly shrank away from the blows of the whip. _(dictionaryapi)_
