## Definition
free or available for use or disposition

## Examples
- every disposable piece of equipment was sent to the fire _(wordnet)_
- disposable assets _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- domain: business
- advanced_form: [[expendable]] — Use 'expendable' when something can be used up or sacrificed, while 'disposable' often implies thrown away after one use; in the sense of available for use, 'expendable' emphasizes being spent without regret.
- advanced_form: [[uncommitted]] — Use 'uncommitted' for resources or time not yet allocated, while 'disposable' in this sense means free to use after necessities (like disposable income); 'uncommitted' stresses lack of prior assignment.
