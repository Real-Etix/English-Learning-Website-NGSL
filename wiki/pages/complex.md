## Definition
a conceptual whole made up of complicated and related parts

## Examples
- the complex of shopping malls, houses, and roads created a new town _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- synonym: [[composite]]
- builds_on: [[structure]] — Use 'complex' instead of 'structure' when referring to a group of related buildings or facilities that form a single entity.
- advanced_form: [[constellation]] — Use 'constellation' to emphasize a group of connected elements that together form a pattern or whole, often suggesting a broader or more abstract set than 'complex'.
- advanced_form: [[aggregate]] — Use 'aggregate' to highlight the total sum or combined whole of distinct parts, especially in formal or technical contexts, whereas 'complex' may imply intricate interconnection.
