## Definition
in the area or vicinity

## Examples
- hanging around _(wordnet)_
- waited around for the next flight _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- synonym: [[about]]
- advanced_form: [[nearby]] — Use instead of 'around' when you mean in the immediate area, not just the general vicinity.
- advanced_form: [[adjacent]] — Use when something is directly next to or adjoining another place, more precise than 'around'.
- advanced_form: [[proximate]] — Use in formal or technical writing to mean very close in location, often implying an effect or relationship.
