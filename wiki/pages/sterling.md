## Definition

## Examples

## Connections
- domain: business
- advanced_form: [[pound]] — Use 'pound' in everyday conversation to refer to the basic unit of British currency; it is more common than 'sterling'.
- advanced_form: [[quid]] — Use 'quid' as an informal, slang term for pound in casual British speech.
- advanced_form: [[sovereign]] — Use 'sovereign' for a historical gold coin worth one pound, not for modern paper money or everyday use.
