## Definition
unusually great in size or amount or degree or especially extent or scope

## Examples
- at vast (or immense) expense _(wordnet)_
- the vast reaches of outer space _(wordnet)_

## Connections

- builds_on: [[large]] — Use for something covering a very large area or extent.
