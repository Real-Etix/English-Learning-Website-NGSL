## Definition
(sometimes followed by `with') in agreement or consistent or reliable

## Examples
- testimony consistent with the known facts _(wordnet)_
- I have decided that the course of conduct which I am following is consistent with my sense of responsibility as president in time of war- FDR _(wordnet)_
