## Definition
showing concern for the rights and feelings of others

## Examples
- Consider that we’ve had three major events and the year has hardly begun. _(dictionaryapi)_
- I’m considering going to the beach tomorrow. _(dictionaryapi)_
- Consider yourself lucky, but consider your opponent skillful. _(dictionaryapi)_
