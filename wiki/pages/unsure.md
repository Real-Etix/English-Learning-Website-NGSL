## Definition
lacking self-confidence

## Examples
- a very unsure young man _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- antonym: [[confident]]
- domain: toeic
- advanced_form: [[insecure]] — Use 'insecure' when the lack of confidence is about self-worth or ability in a general or social sense.
- advanced_form: [[diffident]] — Use 'diffident' to emphasize a shy or reserved lack of confidence, especially in social or professional settings.
- advanced_form: [[hesitant]] — Use 'hesitant' when the lack of confidence leads to delay or reluctance in taking action.
