## Definition
(interrogative) What one or ones (of those mentioned or implied).

## Examples
- By now, you must surely know which is which. _(dictionaryapi)_
- Please take which you please. _(dictionaryapi)_
- He had to leave, which was very difficult. _(dictionaryapi)_
