## Definition
made in or typical of earlier times and valued for its age

## Examples
- the beautiful antique French furniture _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- domain: toeic

- advanced_form: [[historic]] — Use 'historic' instead of 'antique' when the item has historical significance, not just old age.
