## Definition
likely but not certain to be or become true or real

## Examples
- he foresaw a probable loss _(wordnet)_

- antonym: [[unlikely]]

- advanced_form: [[feasible]] — Use 'feasible' when something is possible and practical to achieve, focusing on viability rather than mere likelihood.
