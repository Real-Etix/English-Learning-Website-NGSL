## Definition
(economics) a market in which there are many buyers but only one seller

## Examples
- A land monopoly renders its holder(s) nearly almighty in an agricultural society. _(dictionaryapi)_
- Granting monopolies in concession constitutes a market-conform alternative to taxation for the state, while the crown sometimes bestowed a monopoly as an outrageous gift. _(dictionaryapi)_
