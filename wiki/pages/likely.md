## Definition
has a good chance of being the case or of coming about

## Examples
- these services are likely to be available to us all before long _(wordnet)_
- she is likely to forget _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- antonym: [[unlikely]]
- advanced_form: [[probable]] — Use 'probable' instead of 'likely' when emphasizing a strong chance based on evidence or logic.
- advanced_form: [[plausible]] — Use 'plausible' instead of 'likely' when something seems believable or reasonable, even if not certain.
