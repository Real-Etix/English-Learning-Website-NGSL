## Definition
relating to or applicable to or concerned with the administration of a city or town or district rather than a larger area

## Examples
- local taxes _(wordnet)_
- local authorities _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- antonym: [[national]]
- advanced_form: [[municipal]] — Use 'municipal' instead of 'local' when emphasizing official city or town government authority.
- advanced_form: [[civic]] — Use 'civic' instead of 'local' when focusing on the responsibilities or activities of residents in a town or city.
