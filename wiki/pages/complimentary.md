## Definition
conveying or resembling a compliment

## Examples
- a complimentary remark _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- domain: toeic
- advanced_form: [[laudatory]] — Use 'laudatory' to express strong, formal praise, often in writing or speeches, instead of 'complimentary' which is more casual.
- advanced_form: [[commendatory]] — Use 'commendatory' for official or professional praise, emphasizing approval, rather than the general 'complimentary'.
- advanced_form: [[eulogistic]] — Use 'eulogistic' for highly respectful or eloquent praise, especially in tributes, whereas 'complimentary' is simpler and less formal.
