- advanced_form: [[interlocutor]] — Use 'interlocutor' when you want a formal word for someone you exchange letters with, especially in intellectual or professional correspondence; it emphasizes the exchange of ideas rather than just the act of writing.
- advanced_form: [[penfriend]] — Use 'penfriend' when you refer to a person you regularly write to but have never met in person; it is a standard, single‑word synonym that avoids the ambiguity of 'correspondent' (which can also mean a journalist).
