## Definition
be cognizant or aware of a fact or a specific piece of information

## Examples
- He knew something terrible was going to happen. _(dictionaryapi)_
- Did you know Michelle and Jack were getting divorced? ― Yes, I knew. _(dictionaryapi)_
- I know your mother, but I’ve never met your father. _(dictionaryapi)_
