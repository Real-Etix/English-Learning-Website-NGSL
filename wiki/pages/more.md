## Definition
(comparative of `much' used with mass nouns) a quantifier meaning greater in size or amount or extent or degree

## Examples
- I could no more climb that than fly! _(dictionaryapi)_
- You're more beautiful than I ever imagined. _(dictionaryapi)_
- I was more better at English than you. _(dictionaryapi)_
