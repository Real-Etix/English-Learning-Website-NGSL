## Definition
(of events) having extremely unfortunate or dire consequences

## Examples
- the battle was a disastrous end to a disastrous campaign _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- synonym: [[black]]
- domain: business
- advanced_form: [[catastrophic]] — Use 'catastrophic' instead of 'disastrous' when emphasizing a sudden, large-scale, and overwhelming tragedy, especially in natural disasters or major failures.
- advanced_form: [[calamitous]] — Use 'calamitous' instead of 'disastrous' to stress a deeply distressing or ruinous event, often with a sense of widespread suffering or misfortune.
- advanced_form: [[devastating]] — Use 'devastating' instead of 'disastrous' when focusing on the severe emotional or physical destruction, leaving something in ruins.
