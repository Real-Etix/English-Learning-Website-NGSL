## Definition
get involved, so as to alter or hinder an action, or through force or threat of force

## Examples
- Why did the U.S. not intervene earlier in WW II? _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- synonym: [[interfere]]
- domain: business
- domain: academic
- builds_on: [[interrupt]] — Use 'intervene' when the interruption is intended to stop, prevent, or alter an ongoing action or situation, often with a mediating purpose.
- advanced_form: [[intercede]] — Use 'intercede' when intervening on behalf of someone to help or plead their case.
- advanced_form: [[mediate]] — Use 'mediate' when intervening to help opposing sides reach an agreement.
- advanced_form: [[arbitrate]] — Use 'arbitrate' when intervening officially to make a binding decision.
