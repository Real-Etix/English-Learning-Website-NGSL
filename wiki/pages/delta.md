## Definition
a low triangular area of alluvial deposits where a river divides before entering a larger body of water

## Examples
- the Mississippi River delta _(wordnet)_
- the Nile delta _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- domain: business

- advanced_form: [[fan]] — Use 'fan' for a similar triangular deposit formed by a river in a dry or mountainous area, while 'delta' is specifically at a river's mouth into a body of water.
