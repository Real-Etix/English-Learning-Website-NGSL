## Definition
a specialized division of a large organization

## Examples
- you'll find it in the hardware department _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- synonym: [[section]]
- advanced_form: [[division]] — Use 'division' instead of 'department' when referring to a larger, more autonomous segment within a company or institution, especially in business or military contexts.
- advanced_form: [[sector]] — Use 'sector' instead of 'department' when discussing broad areas of activity (e.g., public sector, private sector) or when emphasizing the functional category rather than internal hierarchy.
- advanced_form: [[bureau]] — Use 'bureau' instead of 'department' in formal or government contexts, or when the unit has a specific, specialized role like a bureau of statistics.
