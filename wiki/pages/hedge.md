## Definition
avoid or try to avoid fulfilling, answering, or performing (duties, questions, or issues)

## Examples
- He trims the hedge once a week. _(dictionaryapi)_
- A hedge is an investment position intended to offset potential losses/gains that may be incurred by a companion investment. In simple language, a hedge is used to reduce any substantial losses/gains suffered by an individual or an organization. _(dictionaryapi)_
