## Definition
religious ministers collectively (especially Presbyterian)  

## Examples
- She works for the ministry of finance. _(dictionaryapi)_
- The premier offered his last ministry's resignation to the monarch, and is asked to form a new one in accordance with the election results. _(dictionaryapi)_
- the present ministry of the Holy Spirit _(dictionaryapi)_
