## Definition
a journey or route all the way around a particular place or area

## Examples
- they took an extended tour of Europe _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- synonym: [[circuit]]
- advanced_form: [[circuit]] — Use 'circuit' instead of 'tour' when the route is round-trip, returning to the starting point.
- advanced_form: [[expedition]] — Use 'expedition' instead of 'tour' when the journey has a specific, often adventurous or research-oriented purpose.
