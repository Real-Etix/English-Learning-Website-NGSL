## Definition
Extremely large in size, amount, or degree.

## Examples
- The stadium is enormous — it seats ninety thousand people. _(dictionaryapi)_
- They spent an enormous amount of money on the project. _(dictionaryapi)_

## Usage note
Use "enormous" when "big" or "large" undersell it. Common with `amount`, `impact`,
`pressure` — abstract quantities, not just physical size.
