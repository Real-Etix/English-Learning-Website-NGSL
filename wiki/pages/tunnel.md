## Definition
a passageway through or under something, usually underground (especially one for trains or cars)

## Examples
- the tunnel reduced congestion at that intersection _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- domain: toeic
- domain: business
- advanced_form: [[underpass]] — Use 'underpass' for a tunnel specifically designed for pedestrians or vehicles to go under a road, while 'tunnel' is more general and can refer to any underground passage.
- advanced_form: [[subway]] — Use 'subway' when referring to an underground railway system or a pedestrian tunnel under a street, especially in urban areas, whereas 'tunnel' is a general term for any underground passage.
- advanced_form: [[gallery]] — Use 'gallery' for a horizontal underground passage in a mine, while 'tunnel' is broader and used for transportation or utility passages.
