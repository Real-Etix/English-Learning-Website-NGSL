## Definition
of concern to or concerning the affairs of other nations (other than your own)

## Examples
- foreign trade _(wordnet)_
- a foreign office _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- antonym: [[domestic]]
- advanced_form: [[alien]] — Use 'alien' instead of 'foreign' when discussing legal or political status of people from other countries, especially in formal or legal contexts.
- advanced_form: [[exogenous]] — Use 'exogenous' instead of 'foreign' when emphasizing influences or factors that originate from outside a system, often in academic or technical writing.
