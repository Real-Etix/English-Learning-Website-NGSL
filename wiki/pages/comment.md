## Definition
a statement that expresses a personal opinion or belief or adds information

## Examples
- from time to time she contributed a personal comment on his account _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- synonym: [[remark]]
- synonym: [[input]]
- advanced_form: [[assertion]] — Use 'assertion' when the comment is stated confidently and forcefully, often with the intent to convince.
- advanced_form: [[contention]] — Use 'contention' when the comment is part of an argument or debate, expressing a point that may be challenged.
