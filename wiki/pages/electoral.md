## Definition
of or relating to elections

## Examples
- Jefferson received 162 electoral votes. _(dictionaryapi)_
- Garfield won 214 electoral votes. _(dictionaryapi)_

## Connections
- domain: business
- advanced_form: [[elective]] — Use 'elective' when emphasizing the act of choosing by vote, often for positions; it is more formal than 'electoral' and can also imply optionality.
- advanced_form: [[psephological]] — Use 'psephological' for academic or analytical contexts about election behavior, more specialized and formal than 'electoral'.
- advanced_form: [[constituent]] — Use 'constituent' when focusing on the voters or electoral districts, rather than the election process itself; it is a more precise term in political contexts.
