## Connections
- synonym: [[discourse]]
- builds_on: [[talk]] — Use 'discuss' when talking about a specific topic in detail, especially in a structured or official setting.
- advanced_form: [[debate]] — Use 'debate' when the discussion involves opposing viewpoints and arguments, unlike 'discuss' which is more neutral.
- advanced_form: [[deliberate]] — Use 'deliberate' when the discussion is careful, slow, and often about making a decision, rather than just exchanging ideas.
- advanced_form: [[confer]] — Use 'confer' when multiple people exchange information or advice to reach a conclusion, often in a formal or professional setting.
