## Definition
compensation paid (to someone) for damages or losses or money already spent etc.

## Examples
- he received reimbursement for his travel expenses _(wordnet)_

## Connections

- builds_on: [[payback]] — Use 'reimbursement' when the repayment covers specific out-of-pocket costs incurred, not necessarily an investment; 'payback' is less precise.
- advanced_form: [[restitution]] — Use when emphasizing restoration of something lost or taken, often in legal contexts.
