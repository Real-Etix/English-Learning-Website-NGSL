## Definition
the psychological feature that arouses an organism to action toward a desired goal

## Examples
- we did not understand his motivation _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- synonym: [[motive]]
- synonym: [[need]]
- advanced_form: [[impetus]] — Use 'impetus' when referring to something that gives energy or momentum to start or accelerate an action, often external.
- advanced_form: [[incentive]] — Use 'incentive' when the motivation is a specific reward or benefit that encourages action.
- advanced_form: [[stimulus]] — Use 'stimulus' when the motivation is an external factor that provokes a response, often in economic or physiological contexts.
