## Definition
the fluid (red in vertebrates) that is pumped through the body by the heart and contains plasma, blood cells, and platelets

## Examples
- blood carries oxygen and nutrients to the tissues and carries away waste products _(wordnet)_
- the ancients believed that blood was the seat of the emotions _(wordnet)_
