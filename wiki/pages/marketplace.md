## Definition
the world of commercial activity where goods and services are bought and sold

## Examples
- Some high-street retailers were slow to enter the new digital marketplace of the Internet. _(dictionaryapi)_

## Connections

- advanced_form: [[mart]] — Use 'mart' as a concise, slightly formal synonym for a market or trading center, often in compound terms like 'trade mart', but less common for the abstract concept of commerce.
