## Definition
move toward, travel toward something or somebody or approach something or somebody

## Examples
- Come with me to the Casbah _(wordnet)_
- come down here! _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- antonym: [[depart]]
- advanced_form: [[approach]] — Use 'approach' instead of 'come' when emphasizing a deliberate or gradual movement toward someone or something.
- advanced_form: [[arrive]] — Use 'arrive' instead of 'come' when reaching a destination or specific place, especially after traveling.
