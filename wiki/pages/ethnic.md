## Definition
denoting or deriving from or distinctive of the ways of living built up by a group of people

## Examples
- influenced by ethnic and cultural ties- J.F.Kennedy _(wordnet)_
- ethnic food _(wordnet)_

## Connections

- advanced_form: [[ancestral]] — Use 'ancestral' to emphasize heritage passed down through generations, while 'ethnic' is broader and refers to shared cultural traits, not necessarily lineage.
- advanced_form: [[indigenous]] — Use 'indigenous' specifically for peoples or cultures native to a region, whereas 'ethnic' can describe any group with shared culture, including immigrant communities.
