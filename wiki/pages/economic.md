## Definition
of or relating to an economy, the system of production and management of material wealth

## Examples
- economic growth _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- synonym: [[economical]]
- advanced_form: [[financial]] — Use 'financial' when focusing on money management and markets, broader than 'economic' which relates to the whole economy.
- advanced_form: [[monetary]] — Use 'monetary' specifically for issues related to money supply, currency, and banking, whereas 'economic' is more general.
- advanced_form: [[fiscal]] — Use 'fiscal' for government revenue, taxation, and spending, while 'economic' covers the entire system including production and consumption.
