## Definition
income from capital investment paid in a series of regular payments

## Examples
- The law enables us to receive an annuity. _(dictionaryapi)_
- If it weren't for her annuity, she'd never be able to pay the bills. _(dictionaryapi)_

## Connections

- collocation: [[never]]
- advanced_form: [[perpetuity]] — Use 'perpetuity' when the annuity has no end date, unlike a standard annuity which may be for a fixed term.
- advanced_form: [[endowment]] — Use 'endowment' when the capital is donated and the income is used for a specific purpose, rather than a personal investment.
- advanced_form: [[pension]] — Use 'pension' specifically for retirement income from an employer, whereas annuity can be purchased privately.
- builds_on: [[pension]] — Use 'annuity' when emphasizing a fixed sum paid annually (or periodically) from a financial product, often more formal than 'pension'.
