## Definition
a kind of hedged investment meant to capture slight differences in price

## Examples
- The boardroom grew restive when the CEO discussed complex arbitrage strategies no one fully understood. _(dictionaryapi)_
- He had been British Consul in Holland, and had seen the workings of the Amsterdam Bourse and the arbitrage business between London and Amsterdam, which was considerable in the middle of the eighteenth century. _(dictionaryapi)_
