## Definition
the probability of a specified outcome  

## Examples
- In all likelihood the meeting will be cancelled. _(dictionaryapi)_
- "There is no likelihood between pure light and black darkness, or between righteousness and reprobation." (Sir W. Raleigh) _(dictionaryapi)_
- "What of his heart perceive you in his face by any likelihood he showed to-day ?" (Shak) _(dictionaryapi)_
