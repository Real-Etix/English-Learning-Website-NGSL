## Definition
money that is risked for possible monetary gain  

## Examples
- He gambled his reputation on the outcome. _(dictionaryapi)_
- I had a gamble on the horses last weekend. _(dictionaryapi)_
- The sailors had taken many gambles with the sea and always won. _(dictionaryapi)_
