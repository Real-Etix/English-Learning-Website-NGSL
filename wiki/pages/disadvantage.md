## Definition
the quality of having an inferior or less favorable position  

## Examples
- They fear it might disadvantage honest participants to allow automated entries. _(dictionaryapi)_
- The disadvantage to owning a food processor is that you have to store it somewhere. _(dictionaryapi)_
- My height is a disadvantage for reaching high shelves. _(dictionaryapi)_
