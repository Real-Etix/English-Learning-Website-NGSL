## Definition
occurring or existing before birth

## Examples
- the prenatal period _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- domain: fitness
- builds_on: [[fetal]] — Use 'prenatal' instead of 'fetal' when referring to the entire period before birth, not just the fetus itself.
- advanced_form: [[antenatal]] — Use 'antenatal' instead of 'prenatal' especially in British English; it conveys the same meaning but is more common in medical contexts.
- advanced_form: [[gestational]] — Use 'gestational' when referring specifically to the period of pregnancy or conditions that arise during that time; it is more precise than 'prenatal'.
