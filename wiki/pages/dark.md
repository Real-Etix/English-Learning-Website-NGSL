## Definition
devoid of or deficient in light or brightness

## Examples
- sitting in a dark corner _(wordnet)_
- a dark day _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- antonym: [[light]]
- advanced_form: [[dim]] — Use 'dim' for a moderate lack of light where details are still faintly visible, often implying reduced brightness rather than complete darkness.
- advanced_form: [[gloomy]] — Use 'gloomy' for a dark, depressing, or shadowy place that evokes a sad or somber mood, beyond just lacking light.
- advanced_form: [[shadowy]] — Use 'shadowy' when darkness is caused by shadows or when something is hard to see because it is obscured by shade or dim light.
