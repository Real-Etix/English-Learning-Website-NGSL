## Definition
to break and turn over earth especially with a plow

## Examples
- I've still got to plough that field. _(dictionaryapi)_
- Some days I have to plough from sunrise to sunset. _(dictionaryapi)_
- Trucks plowed through the water to ferry flood victims to safety. _(dictionaryapi)_
