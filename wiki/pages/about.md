## Definition
(of quantities) imprecise but fairly close to correct

## Examples
- I looked about at the scenery that surrounded me. _(dictionaryapi)_
- Bits of old machinery were lying about. _(dictionaryapi)_
- walking about;  rushing about;  jumping about;  thrashing about _(dictionaryapi)_
