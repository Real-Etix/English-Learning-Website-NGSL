## Definition
a writ from a court commanding police to perform specified acts  

## Examples
- That tree is going to fall, I’ll warrant. _(dictionaryapi)_
- I am warranted to search these premises fully. _(dictionaryapi)_
- Circumstances arose that warranted the use of lethal force. _(dictionaryapi)_
