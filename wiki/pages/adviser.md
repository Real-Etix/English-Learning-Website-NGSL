## Definition
an expert who gives advice

## Examples
- an adviser helped students select their courses _(wordnet)_

## Connections
- synonym: [[advisor]]
- synonym: [[consultant]]
- builds_on: [[mentor]] — Use 'adviser' for a formal role in an institution, whereas 'mentor' suggests a more informal, trusted guide.
- advanced_form: [[consultant]] — Use 'consultant' for a professional paid to give expert advice in a specific field, often more formal than 'adviser'.
- advanced_form: [[counselor]] — Use 'counselor' for someone who offers guidance and support, especially in personal, educational, or therapeutic settings.
- advanced_form: [[mentor]] — Use 'mentor' for a more experienced person who provides long-term guidance and personal development, unlike a general 'adviser'.
