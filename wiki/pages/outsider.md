## Definition
someone who is excluded from or is not a member of a group  

## Examples
- While the initiated easily understand the symbols, they are wholly inaccessible to outsiders. _(dictionaryapi)_
- Seeing the mess professional politicians have made of things is it any wonder the electorate is beginning to prefer outsiders. _(dictionaryapi)_
- Johnny was an outsider at this years karate tournament, but he still managed to win second place out of sheer determination. _(dictionaryapi)_
