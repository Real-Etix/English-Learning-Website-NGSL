## Definition
a sheet or band of fibrous connective tissue separating or binding together muscles and organs etc

## Connections
- domain: fitness
- advanced_form: [[aponeurosis]] — Use 'aponeurosis' instead of 'fascia' when referring specifically to a broad, flat tendon-like sheet that connects muscles to bones or to other muscles, especially in the abdominal wall or scalp.
- advanced_form: [[retinaculum]] — Use 'retinaculum' instead of 'fascia' when describing a thickened band of fascia that functions as a pulley to restrain tendons, commonly found at the wrist or ankle.
